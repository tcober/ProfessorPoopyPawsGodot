; Alembic Town — 56x34 tiles (896x544 px), Basil's origin village, and since
; 2026-07-29 a TWO-STRATA CANOPY TOWN: houses up in the boughs on plank
; platforms, rope bridges between them, the forest floor walkable below, and
; rope ladders plus one rope DINGHY LIFT joining the two. Shared source of
; truth: tiled by assets/_gen_tileset_town.py on the shared OverWorld driver,
; collision/markers by scene/alembic_town.gd.
;
; THERE IS NO ELEVATION SYSTEM AND THERE DOES NOT NEED TO BE ONE. A cell is
; exactly one (x, y) with one legend char and one walk/solid bit, so a body is
; never "on-bridge-or-under-bridge": there IS no cell under a bridge, the deck IS
; the cell. Two storeys are therefore unambiguous IFF THEY ARE DISJOINT IN THE
; GRID, which is exactly the shape of a terrace — two flat walkable regions
; separated by a band of solid cells wearing opaque face art. The `stratum:`
; token below declares which storey a char belongs to and TileScene.assert_strata
; is the whole system: fuse the canopy to the floor with ONE mistyped cell and
; the boardwalk silently stops being a storey and becomes a wooden patch of
; forest, which renders, dedupes, lints clean and ships.
;
; THE CENTRAL AXIS IS GROUND, FLOOR TO GATE, AND THAT IS FORCED. The ground must
; reach both the south gate and the Academy stair, so it cuts the map
; north-south, so THE CANOPY IS PERMANENTLY TWO NETWORKS — west and east — and
; you cross between them on the ground, through the square. Cols 25 and 30 are
; the `a` bough curtains that keep the two strata from touching across it.
;
; THE HOME PLATFORM IS SEVEN ROWS DEEP (13-19) AND THAT IS ARITHMETIC, NOT TASTE.
; scene/town_thesis.gd's `plant` beat walk-gates to `home + (0,26)` and drops
; Schweinler's bag at `home + BAG_OFF (0,38)`, and its `dash` beat walks to
; `home + (0,22)`. With the door at (10,17) — anchor pixel (168,280) — those land
; on rows 19, 19 and 18. Shave a deck row off this platform and three shipped
; cutscenes end early against a wall with no lint and no error.
; TileScene.assert_door_approach enforces two clear rows under EVERY door here.
;
; Geography, depth-first: the barred ACADEMY still crowns the north stone
; terrace on the central axis (rows 0-12 are untouched — the establishment is
; built of cut stone on a cliff; the town is GROWN, and built of wood), and the
; grand stone stair (S) descends through the cliff band (C) to the lamp-flanked
; stair plaza. West canopy: BASIL'S HOUSE (hH3) on its own platform, the rope
; span W1 over a bough mass, then THE BRASS FANG (xX7) — the whole west
; boardwalk, reached by Basil's own ladder (z, cols 8-9) or the square's (Z,
; cols 22-23). East canopy: the DINGHY LIFT's top landing (M) over its shaft
; (Q) and crank drum (I/i) in the square's NE nook, the boardwalk east to THE
; CRACKED FLASK (pP8), a GREAT TREE (j trunk / J decked crown) rising through
; the boards, the HERO SPAN over the creek, and THE LOOKOUT with its bough mass
; (A) where the goose hides. Forest floor: the fountain SQUARE cell-for-cell
; where it always was, the two ground cottages (a neighbour's, and THE DOCTOR'S
; OFFICE — you do not carry a cat who has been run over up a rope ladder), the
; well, THE COPPER KETTLE (nN9) tucked under the east boardwalk at the great
; tree's foot, the creek (r) with its footbridge (=), the fenced orchard, the SE
; pond, and the lamp-and-fence-flanked south gate out to the overworld.
;
; Buildings, trees AND street furniture are Tier-3 y-sorted World sprites from
; town_props.txt (scene/prop_spawner.gd); each building spans THREE chars (solid
; facade + walkable roof + solid body-top digit) only for collision and the
; sprite's footprint bbox. The upper tile layer carries ONLY mask-band depth
; strips and the spans' near rails. Silhouette-fit retypes: a footprint cell
; whose y-sorted art is transparent or a thin walk-behind head is a WALKABLE
; TWIN of its solid char (O/L/U, and the case pairs below) — same terrain name,
; so the paint is identical and only collision changes; the T3 coverage lint in
; _check_art.py keeps the pairing honest.

; `d` is the CANOPY door: the same `door` TERRAIN as D — so every door lint, the
; door-approach assert and the reachability sweep see it — and a different CHAR
; only because a legend char carries exactly one stratum, and three of this
; town's six doors are now up in the boughs.
; B/V and b/v are the same paint; the case pair records the FASCIA HEIGHT, which
; is the one thing about a deck a reader needs and cannot see: a `V` deck sits
; over a 3-row `v` band (a HIGH platform), a `B` deck over a 2-row `b` band.
; stamp_columns asserts those heights, and it is the assert that matters — the
; art is T*run tall and lands on the run's TOP cell, so a short run paints a
; timber wall over the walkable cell below it and nothing else lints that.
; W spans are TWO ROWS DEEP because the rails live in the second row; a 1-cell
; span would put a rail on the walking line. `e` is a span's 1-row fascia, whose
; art goes to the UPPER layer (the shipped bridge_fascia idiom) — the plan below
; a span is always a NAMED POSITIVE THING (`a` bough, `j` trunk, `r` creek),
; never an absence: authoring an absence is the one thing this format is bad at.
; z/Z are `link`, the only stratum allowed to touch two storeys at once, and
; assert_strata requires every link component to border EXACTLY TWO.
; Q is the lift SHAFT: a paint twin of `v` so the boat hangs in front of finished
; fascia art, SOLID because the ride is scripted, and EXCLUDED from mask_band —
; that unmasked 3-cell gap in the railing IS the gate you step through onto the
; boat.
