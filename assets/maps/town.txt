; Alembic Town — 72x48 tiles (1152x768 px), Basil's origin village, and since
; 2026-07-30 a FOUR-STOREY CANOPY VILLAGE: the Academy's cut-stone terrace over
; the homes in the crowns over the public town on the low boardwalk over the
; forest floor. Tiled by assets/_gen_tileset_town.py on the shared OverWorld
; driver; collision/markers by scene/alembic_town.gd.
;
; THERE IS NO ELEVATION SYSTEM AND THERE DOES NOT NEED TO BE ONE. A cell is
; exactly one (x, y) with one legend char and one walk/solid bit, so a body is
; never "on-bridge-or-under-bridge": there IS no cell under a bridge, the deck IS
; the cell. Storeys are unambiguous IFF THEY ARE DISJOINT IN THE GRID, which is
; exactly the shape of a terrace. The `stratum:` token declares which storey a
; walkable char belongs to and TileScene.assert_strata is the whole system: fuse
; two storeys with ONE mistyped cell and the upper one silently stops being a
; storey, and it renders, dedupes, lints clean and ships.
;
; THE TRUNKS ARE THE ARMATURE AND THE TOWN HANGS ON THEM. Five great trees, each
; a FIVE-column channel, and the trunk's solid footprint is only the middle three
; — so the deck closes north, south, east and west of every shaft and YOU CAN WALK
; THE FULL CIRCLE AROUND EVERY TREE, PASSING BEHIND IT, on all three walkable
; storeys. That ring is the single most recognisable thing about Slitherbough and
; the Ewok village, and it is why the channel is 5 wide and not 3: a 3-col channel
; is all trunk, and a trunk you cannot walk round is a pillar.
;
; Per channel, north to south — and every walkable row here is part of a ring:
;   14-16  g  the CROWN's leaf mass, all 5 cols   WALK canopy_hi   north arc
;   17-18  J  the shaft, cols 1-3                 solid
;   17-18  Y  the side arcs, cols 0 and 4         WALK canopy_hi
;   19-20  Y  the through-lane, all 5 cols        WALK canopy_hi   south arc
;   21-23  %  the high fascia, trunk crossing it  solid  (paint twin of y)
;   24-25  B  north arc                           WALK canopy_lo
;   26-27  j  the shaft, cols 1-3                 solid
;   28-30  B  the through-lane                    WALK canopy_lo   south arc
;   31-33  &  the low fascia, trunk crossing it   solid  (paint twin of v)
;   34-35  .  north arc                           WALK ground
;   36-38  !  the FOOT + buttress roots, cols 1-3 solid
;   39+    .  the floor                           WALK ground
; ONE SPRITE PER SEGMENT, because one sprite cannot depth-sort against bodies on
; two storeys at once — see _tree_props.great_trunk. A single trunk sprite keyed at
; its foot draws over a body standing anywhere above it, and since a shaft is an
; opaque mass that body is not "behind" anything, it is GONE. That shipped once.
;
; THE ROW BUDGET IS ARITHMETIC, NOT TASTE. scene/town_thesis.gd walk-gates to
; `home + (0,26)` and drops Schweinler's bag at `home + (0,38)`; with Basil's door
; on row 18 those land on rows 20 and 20, both inside the high lane. The forecourt
; is three rows because the dash goal is a 48x16 Area2D at `school + (0,24)`.
; Every fascia is exactly 3 rows because stamp_columns asserts the run height.
; Shave a row anywhere and three shipped cutscenes end early against a wall with
; no lint and no error. TileScene.assert_door_approach enforces two clear rows
; under EVERY door.
;
; THE STAIR IS A LINK, NOT GROUND. It joins the Academy's forecourt to canopy_hi,
; so you reach the establishment from the TOP of the town rather than from the
; lane — which is why the canopy no longer has to be cut in two the way the
; two-strata version was, and why every storey is one connected network.
;
; Geography: the barred ACADEMY crowns its stone terrace (the forecourt is cut
; into the hillside, so the C band exists only where the terrace does). CANOPY
; HIGH is the homes — BASIL'S HOUSE in bay A at the west end, two neighbours east,
; the hero span over a bough mass in bay B, and the stair plaza in bay C. CANOPY
; LOW is the public town — THE BRASS FANG, THE CRACKED FLASK (adult Sage's shop),
; THE COPPER KETTLE, a second span in bay A, and the DINGHY LIFT's top landing in
; bay E over its solid shaft and crank drum. THE FOREST FLOOR keeps the OLD
; village: the fountain square you arrive into from the gate, THE DOCTOR'S OFFICE
; with its clear lane east, the neighbour's cottage, the well, the market stall,
; the creek and its footbridge, the fenced orchard, the SE pond, and the
; lamp-flanked south gate. A second mouth leaves east at rows 42-43 for beat 5b.
;
; Buildings, trees AND street furniture are Tier-3 y-sorted World sprites from
; town_props.txt; each building spans THREE chars (solid facade + roof + ridge
; digit) only for collision and the sprite's footprint bbox. The upper tile layer
; carries ONLY the spans' near rails. A footprint cell whose art is transparent is
; a WALKABLE TWIN of its solid char (O/U/L and the case pairs) — same terrain name,
; so the paint is identical and only collision changes.
