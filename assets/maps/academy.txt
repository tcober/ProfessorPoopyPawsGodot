; THE ALEMBIC ACADEMY — 64x48 tiles (1024x768 px), its own scene since 2026-07-30.
; Reached by the north lane out of Alembic Town; its great door opens into the
; lecture hall (scene/hall.tscn), which is where Prologue B names him.
;
; COMPOSED DEPTH-FIRST ON ONE AXIS, south to north, because that is what makes a
; building epic rather than merely large: the causeway, the beck and its bridge,
; the outer ward, THE RAMPART and its barred gatehouse, the inner court with the
; orrery standing in the middle of it, the grand stair, and THE KEEP on the back
; rank with the rose window over its sealed door. You arrive at the bottom of a
; sequence and the thing you came to see is at the top of it.
;
; THE CAUSEWAY MOUTH IS SEALED (2026-07-30). The last two rows used to be open
; lawn from border to border, so a body could walk off the bottom of the map
; anywhere along it — the scene only walls the exit lane itself. Nothing caught it
; because assets/maps/academy.txt was never in _check_art.py's MAPS list at all:
; the whole precinct shipped without a single map lint looking at it, and the
; summary still printed "all checks passed". A map that is not in the table is not
; passing, it is ABSENT.
;
; THE BECK IS WATER, NOT A CHASM. Verticality here is authoring, and authoring an
; ABSENCE is the one thing this kit is bad at — see the map-authoring skill:
; Lanternwood's rift was attempted twice and read as a wall both times. A stream
; is a POSITIVE thing, it animates on four frames for free, and a bridge over it
; is one more gate on the way in.
;
; THE ORRERY STANDS ON THE AXIS, blocking the straight line from the gate to the
; stair deliberately. You walk round a monument; being made to go round it is what
; makes the court read as the middle of a place rather than as a corridor.
;
; THE KEEP'S CORNER TOWERS ARE THE TWO GREAT TREES, and that is why it is ONE
; 22x9 block (`Aa`, cols 20-41) rather than a keep with a tree either side of it.
; A tree read as a tower has to lean its crown over the hall's roof, and one char
; per cell means a separate crown block never can — so the trees moved INTO the
; keep's footprint and into its sprite, where the pitch runs out and dies into each
; trunk. See _academy_props.academy_keep. The art is 26 cells wide over those 22,
; hanging two cells of leaf into the lawn either side: emit_prop centres art on its
; block, and a two-cell hidden run is a step you pass through (the walk-behind
; visibility lint condemns three).
;
; EVERYTHING ON THE AXIS IS ON x=496 — the gate GG, the stair SS, the orrery and
; now the keep's own door DD. The old keep sat one cell east of its own stair.
;
; EVERY PROP STANDING ON PAVING HAS ITS OWN TERRAIN NAME. A cell paints its
; terrain's render class as underlay, so `lamp` (which renders grass, because
; Alembic's lamps stand on grass) would punch a grass square through the court.
; Hence courtlamp / rampart / gate / towerbody / orrery / orrerybase, all of
; which render `road`.
;
; Tiled by assets/_gen_tileset_academy.py on the shared OverWorld driver;
; collision and markers by scene/academy.gd.
